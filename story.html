OUR STORY

FORGED BY PASSION. BUILT FOR CHAMPIONS.

In the world of esports, success isn't defined by a single victory—it's built through discipline, teamwork, relentless practice, and the courage to compete against the best. TEAM SHIVAA was founded on these principles with a mission to create an organization where aspiring gamers can transform their passion into excellence.

Established by Shiva Tiwari, TEAM SHIVAA began with a bold vision: to build a professional esports organization that nurtures talent, inspires confidence, and represents the next generation of competitive gaming. More than just an esports team, TEAM SHIVAA is a community where players, creators, and fans come together to learn, grow, and achieve greatness.

Every achievement starts with preparation. Under the leadership of Head Coach Rachit Pandey, our athletes receive structured coaching, strategic game analysis, performance reviews, and personalized improvement plans. We believe that true champions are created through consistency, discipline, and a commitment to continuous learning.

A successful esports organization is powered not only by skilled players but also by a strong and united community. Community Manager Ayushi Pandey leads initiatives that strengthen the connection between our players, supporters, and gaming enthusiasts through tournaments, online events, social engagement, and community programs. Her focus is to ensure that every member feels valued and inspired to contribute to the TEAM SHIVAA journey.

At TEAM SHIVAA, we strive to deliver more than competitive success. We are committed to developing leadership, teamwork, sportsmanship, and professionalism—qualities that extend far beyond the gaming arena. Every tournament, every training session, and every challenge is an opportunity to become stronger than yesterday.

As we continue to grow, our vision remains clear: to establish TEAM SHIVAA as one of India's most respected esports organizations, recognized for competitive excellence, innovation, and an unwavering commitment to its community.

Our Mission

To empower students and gamers by providing world-class coaching, competitive opportunities, and a supportive environment where dedication transforms into achievement.

Our Vision

To become a globally recognized esports organization that develops champions, builds meaningful communities, and inspires the future of competitive gaming.

Our Core Values

Excellence We pursue the highest standards in everything we do—from gameplay and coaching to community engagement.
Discipline Consistent effort, structured training, and a champion's mindset are the foundation of long-term success.
Innovation We embrace new strategies, technologies, and ideas to stay ahead in the ever-evolving esports industry.
Teamwork Great victories are achieved through trust, communication, and unity.
Respect Every player, supporter, partner, and opponent deserves respect both inside and outside the game.
